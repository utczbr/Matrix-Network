1. **Locked `omnetpp.ini` TDMA Superframe & $K$ Provisioning (`tab:tdma_feasibility`, §4.2):**
   - Earlier working text reported heuristic superframe durations (`10/10/32/52/90 ms` at `12 Mbps`, `10/10/10/20/48 ms` at `54 Mbps`) from an earlier planning table. The actual pinned `simulations/omnetpp.ini` parameters use $K \in \{1, 1, 2, 8, 39\}$ AP slots ($\tau_{\mathrm{AP}} = \qty{400}{\micro\second}$, $\Tsuper \in \{3, 5, 9, 20, 48\}\,\text{ms}$) at `54 Mbps` and $K \in \{1, 1, 3, 10, 39\}$ ($\tau_{\mathrm{AP}} = \qty{560}{\micro\second}$, $\Tsuper \in \{6, 10, 20, 42, 90\}\,\text{ms}$) at `12 Mbps`, padded to an integer millisecond boundary (`0.16–2.40 ms` idle slack).
   - Consequently, `N=40` at `12 Mbps` ($\Tsuper = \qty{42}{\milli\second} < \qty{50}{\milli\second}$) is structurally feasible for uplink State (`0.00%` misses across all eight loads), and the `-4.58 pp` Orders penalty stems from finite-$K$ ($K=10$, $\lambda=8.40$ orders/cycle, `22.57%` overflowing cycles, `6.54%` single-cycle packet overflow, `4.59%` steady-state $M/D^{10}/1$ `Miss_B`), not $\Tsuper > D_c$.
   - At `N=80` (`12 Mbps`), the merged AP block has `K = 39` slots (`21.84 ms`), not `8` slots (`6.80 ms`), and the `31.89%` Orders `Miss_B` decomposes into `15.2 pp` AP-block phase wait, `4.3 pp` within-block serialization, and `12.5 pp` multi-cycle backlog carryover.
